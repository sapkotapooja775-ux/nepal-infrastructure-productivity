# Public Infrastructure Investment and Regional Economic Productivity in Nepal

## Overview

This project investigates the relationship between public infrastructure investment and regional economic productivity across Nepal's seven provinces.

It combines panel data econometric analysis with Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) to estimate infrastructure productivity relationships and develop evidence-based public investment allocation strategies.

## Research Objectives

- Examine the relationship between public infrastructure investment and provincial GDP.
- Estimate the productivity effects of road, electricity, and other infrastructure.
- Analyse regional differences in infrastructure productivity.
- Estimate marginal economic benefits associated with infrastructure investment.
- Integrate econometric estimates into an investment allocation optimisation model.
- Evaluate alternative public investment scenarios.

## Methodology

**Stage 1: Econometric Analysis**

Panel data regression models, including pooled OLS, fixed effects, and random effects, are used to investigate infrastructure-productivity relationships.

**Stage 2: Investment Optimisation**

Estimated marginal productivity benefits inform a mathematical optimisation model that allocates public investment across provinces and infrastructure sectors.

## Study Area

Nepal's seven provinces:

- Koshi
- Madhesh
- Bagmati
- Gandaki
- Lumbini
- Karnali
- Sudurpashchim
